PHASE 0 – MVP: Free While You Test (You as the Host)

Goal: Prove the concept, refine the product, and observe real user behaviour before charging anyone.

✅ What to focus on:

Host-first experience: You create events, share links, track what guests owe, and optionally collect deposits or payments.

Pain solved: The 20–30 minute chaos at the end of group meals is eliminated. Guests know what they owe, and you know who’s paid before the bill arrives.

Payments optional: Guests can pay by bank transfer, cash, or however they prefer — SplitDine doesn’t need to handle money yet.

Data gathering: Use these events to understand how people interact, where they drop off, what features they use most, and what they ignore.

✅ Actions during this phase:

Host 3–5 of your own meals (even if you don’t attend every one).

Collect direct feedback from guests and note where friction exists.

Refine UI/UX based on real use, not theory.

Build screenshots, testimonials, and real-world examples for marketing later.

PHASE 1 – Early Users: Meetup Organisers & Community Hosts

Goal: Turn SplitDine from a personal tool into something other organisers use.

🎯 Who you’re targeting:

Meetup organisers

Supper club hosts

Event planners for casual dinners, book clubs, language exchanges, etc.

Anyone who regularly organises group meals and hates chasing money or doing maths at the end.

✅ Core value for them:

Save time: No more spreadsheets or calculators — they know who’s paid and how much in real time.

Avoid stress: Deposits reduce last-minute cancellations and no-shows.

Look organised: They appear more professional to their groups.

End meals faster: No more 30-minute end-of-meal delays.

💡 Product features that matter most:

Event creation & sharing links

Guest RSVP + payment tracking

Optional deposits & reminders

Live totals and “who’s paid” view

Exportable summaries or receipts

💸 Monetisation options here:

Freemium model: Free basic events, but paid for unlimited events or premium features (e.g. deposits, custom reminders, co-hosting).

Per-event fee: £0.50–£1 per guest per event.

Monthly subscription: £4.99–£9.99/month for organisers who run multiple meals.

✅ Once ~5–10 hosts are actively using it (without you holding their hand), you know you’ve reached product–market fit for organisers.

PHASE 2 – Restaurant-Facing Landing Page & White-Label Solution

Goal: Expand SplitDine from an organiser tool into a restaurant solution.

🎯 The restaurant problem:

Group bookings are often handled by email and spreadsheets.

Collecting deposits is manual and time-consuming.

Guests change orders and totals constantly.

Large tables block staff and slow down service when splitting bills.

✅ The value proposition for restaurants:

Reduce admin: No more spreadsheets or payment chasing.

Secure bookings: Collect deposits or prepayments automatically.

Speed up service: Final bill is ready when the group arrives — no 10 separate card payments at the till.

Improve guest experience: Groups leave happier and faster, freeing tables for the next booking.

🛠 What the product could offer:

Restaurant-branded event pages (white-label version)

Deposit collection and order tracking

One single payout instead of 10 individual card charges

Dashboard to view guest names, RSVPs, payments, and dietary notes

💸 Monetisation options here:

SaaS subscription (£30–£150/month per venue)

Per booking fee (£1–£2 per guest)

Hybrid model (free for organisers, paid for restaurants)

✅ At this point, SplitDine evolves from “a tool for hosts” → a platform restaurants actively recommend to their group bookings.

📊 Timeline (Realistic Stages)
Phase	Milestone	Focus

1. Organisers	3–6 months	Start onboarding Meetup and community hosts. Add premium features. Test pricing.

📌 Key Principles to Stick To

Host-first, always: Everything should make their job easier. They are your first paying customers.

Guests are secondary: They benefit from clarity and fairness, but they’re not who you sell to.

Restaurants come later: They become your B2B revenue layer once the product is proven.

Payments are the long-term play: Don’t rush them — build trust and adoption first.

Platform, not feature: Always position SplitDine as “how group meals are organised”, not just a calculator.

✨ Long-Term Vision (What Success Looks Like)

Hosts across Meetup, WhatsApp, and supper clubs use SplitDine to run every meal they organise.

Guests associate SplitDine with “fast, fair, and painless” group payments.

Restaurants recommend it for large tables because it saves them time and labour.

Eventually, SplitDine handles the entire group dining lifecycle: RSVPs → deposits → menu choices → payments → receipts.
